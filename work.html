LASU Connect

Campus platform, built solo

Node.js/Express backend, Socket.io for real-time features, MongoDB/Mongoose for data, React/TypeScript frontend. The biggest lesson: real-time features are easy to demo and hard to keep consistent once more than a few users are connected at once — most of the later iteration went into making Socket.io state actually reliable, not into new features.
